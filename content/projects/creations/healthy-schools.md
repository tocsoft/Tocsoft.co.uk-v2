_This website is no longer live, as the programme was significantly reduced following policy changes._

This was a large-scale enterprise web application serving schools across England and Wales. It provided a self-validation and tracking platform designed to improve the health and wellbeing of schools and pupils, with extensive reporting and a strict hierarchical security model.

Inherited as a hybrid VB.NET and C# WebForms application, it was gradually refactored and rewritten into a multi-system C# MVC application capable of handling approximately 20,000 page views per day.

Key features I directly developed included full-text indexed resource search, assessment tools, and a custom management information and business intelligence reporting system.
